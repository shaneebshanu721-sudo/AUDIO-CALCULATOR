        Voice tips
        • Say an amount, pause briefly, say the next. No need to say “plus”.
        • Fast mode: “588 … 1400 … 600” — leave a short pause between amounts.
        • Most reliable for fast work: say digits — “four two zero zero” or “four two double zero” = ₹4,200.
        • If an amount looks wrong, open Details on the tape to see exactly what the phone heard.
        • Set a leaf: “leaf 25” — the next amount goes to leaf 25, then 26, 27…
        • Leaf and amount together: “leaf 7, five hundred eighty eight”.
        • Say “undo” to reverse the last action.
        • Mixed Malayalam/English: try മലയാളം first; if numbers come out wrong, switch to English.
        • Lakh and crore: “one lakh twenty five thousand”, “1.25 lakh”.
